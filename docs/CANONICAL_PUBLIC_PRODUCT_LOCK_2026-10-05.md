# eCommPilot Public Website Product Lock — 2026-10-05

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Applies to:** ecommpilot.net public positioning, navigation, homepage, catalog conversion and future public-site work

eCommPilot is an **eBay dropshipping operating platform**, not a generic research-tool website.

Core promise:

> **You bring your eBay account. eCommPilot gives you ready-to-list products, verified suppliers, profitability calculations, optimized listing content and ongoing product monitoring.**

## Locked navigation

**Winning Products | What's Trending | How It Works | Pricing | Learn | Login | Get Started**

## Logged-out product experience

Public visitors may see safe fields such as:

- product image/title;
- market/category;
- legitimate 30-day sold metric when available;
- active listings;
- recommended selling price;
- estimated profit/margin/ROI;
- delivery;
- safe supplier/provider label;
- freshness/verification.

Never expose:

- exact AliExpress URL;
- supplier external IDs/variant IDs;
- private quote IDs;
- exact protected supplier cost/freight when the app contract keeps them private;
- backup supplier;
- operational evidence/workspace IDs;
- raw provider payloads.

Use Login/Join as the conversion boundary for protected member supplier access.

## What's Trending

This is a secondary engagement tool, not the main product.

Simple UX:

**Market -> Category -> Filters -> Search**

The existing research/discovery infrastructure may support it. Do not turn the homepage into a complicated research dashboard.

## eBay Profit Calculator

Provide a free calculator using:

**Selling Price + Product Cost + Shipping + eBay Fees + optional ad/tax/other costs -> Profit + Margin + ROI**

## Marketing rule

Lead with the problems eBay dropshippers actually have:

- finding products;
- knowing whether they sell;
- finding/validating suppliers;
- calculating profit;
- creating listings;
- monitoring supplier changes;
- tracking listed products/orders/profit.

Then show how eCommPilot removes that work.

## Relationship with app.ecommpilot.net

Normal member app navigation is:

**Winning Products -> My Products -> Listed -> Orders -> Alerts -> What's Trending**

Internal Product Discovery/Research/Suppliers/qualification workflows are admin/operator implementation and must not be marketed as the normal member workflow.

## Change control

Do not revert to generic-tools-first positioning or the old internal research workflow without a new explicit owner decision.

Public API privacy, SEO truthfulness, accessibility and no-fabricated-data rules remain mandatory.
