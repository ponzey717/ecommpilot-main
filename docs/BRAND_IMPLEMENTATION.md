# eCommPilot Brand Implementation

## Current approved direction

Brand style:

**Modern SaaS + marketplace/catalog hybrid**

Primary positioning:

> Winning Products for eBay Dropshippers

Supporting direction:

> Research-backed products, supplier data and ready-to-list information for eBay US, UK and AU.

## Approved logo direction

The approved mark uses:

- a modern shopping cart;
- speed/motion cues;
- an upward arrow/paper-plane form;
- blue/cyan gradients;
- horizontal wordmark `eCommPilot`.

Required asset family:

- horizontal logo;
- square logo;
- icon-only/favicon;
- light-background variant;
- dark-background variant;
- eventual monochrome variant.

Typography direction:

- Inter body/UI.

## UI direction

Use the logo's energy, but the product experience must stay professional and data-led.

The production UI should combine:

- a polished SaaS shell;
- strong marketplace/product discovery;
- prominent product imagery;
- clear metrics;
- restrained gradients;
- obvious profit/sales/delivery hierarchy;
- simple member CTAs.

Avoid:

- generic dropshipping hype;
- excessive gradients across every component;
- cartoon ecommerce art;
- fake testimonials/metrics;
- visually noisy dashboard patterns.

## Theme Studio

The Theme Studio is the visual comparison/prototyping surface.

Do not copy a theme into production until the final choice is explicitly locked.

When choosing between alternatives, evaluate them on:

1. logo compatibility;
2. professional credibility;
3. Winning Products browseability;
4. product card clarity;
5. profit/sales visibility;
6. mobile behavior;
7. CTA hierarchy;
8. SEO content readability;
9. accessibility/contrast;
10. ability to scale to member/admin surfaces.

The chosen theme should be translated into reusable production design tokens rather than copied as one-off CSS.
